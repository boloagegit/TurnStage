## Configure the flow

Work through the Profile Configuration sections in order:

1. **General** — identity and environment.
2. **Opening & Flow** — opening message, variants, stop, and recovery.
3. **Request** — endpoint, headers, body, timeouts, retries, and redirects.
4. **Stream & Mapping** — convert backend events into chat events.
5. **Chat UI** — preview layout and interaction behavior.
6. **History & Errors** and **Security** — retention and allowlists.

Use the redacted request preview before sending production-shaped traffic.
